🕵️ IMPOSTER

Who's faking it?

Everyone gets the same secret word — except the imposter. Describe, discuss, and vote them out. One phone, pass it around.

📖 How to play

1. Pass the phone — each player secretly peeks at their card. Crew members see the secret word; the imposter sees only a hint (if enabled).
2. Going around, everyone says one word or clue about the secret word — vague enough that the imposter can't steal it, clear enough to prove you know it.
3. Discuss, accuse, then vote someone out. Catch all imposters and the crew wins. If imposters survive till they equal the crew — they win. A caught imposter gets one last chance to guess the word and steal the win!
